Blend in or Bust v0.19.26 - Role Gate and Sound Toggle Hotfix

ROLE SCREEN
- Players still receive 12 seconds to read their private Blender or Buster role.
- The role card and Continue button now show a visible automatic countdown.
- Pressing Continue still confirms immediately.
- After 12 seconds, the server releases the role-confirmation gate automatically.
- A student who refuses to press Continue can no longer hold the whole class in Loading Museum.
- The server still waits for each active player's museum to finish loading, protecting slower devices from losing game time behind a loading screen.

SOUND TOGGLE
- Restored pointer/click input to the sound button while it is inside the in-game HUD.
- The button now clearly displays SOUND ON / SOUND OFF.
- The saved sound preference is retained on the device.
- Muting now applies to background music, meeting music, flag sounds, Bust sounds and crime-scene alert sounds.
- Sound effects already playing are stopped when sound is muted.

VERSION
- Updated project and itch build helper version to v0.19.26.
