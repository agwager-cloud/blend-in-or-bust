Blend In or Bust v0.20.0 - Live Maths Flag Challenges

CLIENT
- Removed temporary host-lobby ? question test button.
- Live Blender flag discoveries now open a random 5-choice Maths question.
- Correct answers collect the flag; wrong answers lock A-E for 10 seconds.
- Other Blenders near an active flag challenge see VIEW ? and can open it read-only.
- Question overlay remains responsive on phone, iPad and laptop.
- Correct answers are no longer included in the browser question-bank bundle; validation is server-authoritative.
- Added 10 minute host round option.
- Host Buster selector now supports Automatic or 1-6 Busters.

SERVER
- Server owns active Maths challenge/question/answer validation.
- Human flags cannot be collected by walking over them; a correct Maths answer is required.
- Busters can lift a prop hiding a flag without triggering or being told about its Maths challenge.
- After a wrong answer, disconnect/refresh/quit ends that participant session; rejoining during the active round is CCTV spectator only.
- Automatic balance is 1 public Buster for each complete group of 4 connected participants, with a minimum of 1 in multiplayer and a cap of 6 (24 players => 6 Busters / 18 Blenders).
- 10 minute rounds use expanded flag and rubbish counts for larger classes.

DEPLOY ORDER
1. Deploy the updated server to Render first.
2. Wait until Render reports the deploy is live.
3. Upload the v0.20.0 itch.io client ZIP.
